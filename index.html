How we work

We have been fabricating custom steel work for 4x4 vehicles since 1995. Every cattle frame, bush bar, and game viewing build is made to fit your vehicle — measured, welded, and finished in our workshop at 69 Graham Rd, Shere, Pretoria East.

Tell us what you need on the phone or WhatsApp. We build cattle frames, safari conversions, bush bars, side steps, and one-off pipe work for bakkies, Land Cruisers, Defenders, and off-road rigs.
